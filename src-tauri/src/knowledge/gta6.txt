GRAND THEFT AUTO VI (GTA 6): background you can rely on. Facts are from Rockstar Games' own reveals and trailers; anything a speaker says beyond this is rumour or opinion, so never present it as fact.

The game
- Grand Theft Auto VI (also written GTA 6, GTA VI) is made by Rockstar Games and published by Take-Two Interactive.
- Platforms: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Release date: November 19, 2026 (it moved from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, then to November 19, 2026).
- Trailer 1 came out in December 2023 and broke view records. Trailer 2 came out in May 2025. A further "extended look" was announced for 2026.
- The first trailer used "Love Is a Long Road" by Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers.

Setting
- The state of Leonida: a fictional, Florida-like state. Regions named by Rockstar: Vice City (the Miami-like main city), the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers (swamp country, alligators), Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.
- Vice City is the series' returning city (it first appeared in GTA: Vice City, 2002). Beaches, neon, nightclubs, boats, swamps, heat and "Florida Man" chaos are the visual themes.

Main characters (the two protagonists are playable)
- Lucia Caminos: the first female lead of a main GTA game. She leaves prison and is shown in a Bonnie-and-Clyde style partnership.
- Jason Duval: the second protagonist, shown doing small jobs and driving around Vice City before working with Lucia.
- Named in the reveals: Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike and Dre'Quan Priest (business partners in the music scene), the Real Dimez (a female rap duo on Only Raw Records), Brian Heder.
